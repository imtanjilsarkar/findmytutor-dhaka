📖 About FindmyTutor Dhaka

Who We Are

FindmyTutor Dhaka is Bangladesh's most innovative tuition media platform, dedicated to connecting qualified tutors with students across all areas of Dhaka city. Founded with a vision to revolutionize the tuition industry, we leverage technology to make the matching process seamless, transparent, and efficient.

Our platform serves thousands of students, parents, and tutors, helping them find the perfect educational match based on subjects, location, availability, and teaching preferences.

Our Mission

To provide every student in Dhaka access to quality education by connecting them with verified, qualified tutors.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted tuition media platform in Bangladesh, empowering educators and learners.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, quality education, and customer satisfaction above everything.
